## What happens next?

A clear, structured path from business challenge to working solution:

**1. Discover** → we analyze your business, processes and growth constraints.
**2. Define** → we turn findings into a plan: scope, architecture, timeline, budget.
**3. Develop** → we design and build, with full visibility into progress.
**4. Deliver** → we deploy, adapt to real conditions and evolve the system as you grow.

Smaller systems and MVPs typically ship in **8–12 weeks**; larger ones are delivered in stages for predictable progress.
